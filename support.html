Support

Heldu Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas: write to feedback@heldu.app. A reply usually comes within a few days.

Helpful to include

  • What you did and what you expected to see.
  • The stop, station or route involved, and roughly when.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.

Why a departure or station shows "no data"

Heldu never shows a made-up departure. When a live feed is out of date, it says "no data" until the feed recovers.

Data sources

The full list of data sources and licences is in the app, under Settings.
