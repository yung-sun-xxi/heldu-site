Privacy

Heldu Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Heldu has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not collect personal data about you, and it does not sell or share any.

Location

Heldu asks for your location while you use the app, to show you on the map, to find stops and stations near you and to plan trips from where you are. If you start a trip, it keeps using your location in the background until the trip ends, and not otherwise. Your location is not stored on any server.

What leaves your phone

  • Route planning and timetables. The app sends the start and destination of a route, and the stops you open, to the Heldu server, which answers from public timetable data. The server keeps no log of requests, so it holds no history of your trips.
  • Live bus and metro arrivals, and Bicing and AMBici stations. The app asks the operators' public services (TMB, Bicing, AMBici) directly, for the stop or station you look at. Like any internet request, these reach those services with your IP address, under their own policies.
  • Map and place search. The map and its search are Apple Maps, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Stored on your device only

Your settings, the modes you picked, recent searches, favourites and technical diagnostics (crash and performance reports, a short app log) stay on your phone. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

During a trip, Heldu can notify you, for example when a faster option appears or a return station fills up. These notifications are produced on your phone.

Children

Heldu collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
